# ENCOUNTER — Civilisations, power, money, and durable peace

`kingdom.encounter/0.1` · companion to `kingdom.foundation/0.2` · uses
`kingdom.freedom/0.1` and `kingdom.isness/0.1`

This companion prepares the KINGDOM for contact with other domains, orders,
institutions, and civilisations. It treats contact as a problem of translation,
interdependence, incompatible rules, coercive pressure, and—only in the narrow
case where it is actually present—armed attack. It does not presume a destined
clash.

It is a map for restraint and preparedness, not a manual for conquest. It
contains no targeting method, weapons instruction, covert-action playbook,
propaganda recipe, or authority to fight. `兵法不是國法`: military method is
not a civil constitution. `兵法` may test contingencies at a bounded conflict
edge; `政治` orders shared public life under standing, consent, law,
distribution, appeal, and repair. The first may never silently consume the
second.

The historical source atlas is [CIVILISATIONS.md](CIVILISATIONS.md). It is
representative rather than exhaustive. Its cases are evidence and
counterevidence for institutional design; they are not rankings of peoples,
recipes to copy, or proof that a long-lived order was just.

This document is a companion. It changes no Foundation commitment, changes no
FREEDOM or ISNESS distinction, adds no conformance law, and grants no authority.

## Reading discipline

“Civilisation,” “dynasty,” “empire,” “state,” “domain,” and “people” name
different and contested units. None is automatically a being. None has one
mind simply because a record gives it one name. Every claim should preserve at
least:

```text
(speaker or source,
 statement kind,
 unit and period,
 evidence,
 confidence,
 affected and omitted parties,
 limits,
 correction path)
```

Historical archives are asymmetric. Courts, tax offices, armies, creditors,
monuments, and literate elites leave more records than households, women,
children, enslaved people, pastoral communities, displaced people, dissenters,
or ecological losses. An assessed tax is not a collected tax. A law is not its
implementation. Silence in an archive is unknown, not consent or absence.

The atlas marks evidence, contested interpretation, and bounded modern
inference separately. A repeated pattern may justify a question or a guard; it
does not become a law of history.

## E1 — Civilisation is not a unitary being

A civilisation is an analytical grouping of changing people, institutions,
languages, practices, territories, memories, and material systems. Its border
depends on the question and the source. A dynasty can end while law, cities,
ritual, currency habits, or administration continue. A state can persist while
many of its people are harmed.

Therefore a civilisation has no inferred collective mind, guilt, intention,
desire, or destiny. Statements such as “Rome wanted,” “China believed,” or
“the West chose” are compressed attributions. They must be unpacked into named
actors, institutions, periods, evidence, dissent, and limits.

No ancestry, language, religion, citizenship, or association makes a person
responsible for an institution's act. Standing remains person-by-person and is
not revoked by an assigned group label.

**Invariant E1:** A civilisation is a contested analytical grouping, never one inferred being, will, moral rank, enemy, or bearer of collective guilt.

## E2 — Encounter is typed, not destined enmity

An encounter begins by classifying what is actually observed, while retaining
`unknown` where evidence is insufficient:

```text
encounter_type ∈ {
  exchange,
  interdependence,
  incompatibility,
  coercion,
  armed_attack,
  unknown
}
```

These types are not an escalation ladder. Exchange can coexist with
incompatibility; interdependence can create leverage; an allegation of coercion
can be contested; attack is a factual and legal question, not a metaphor for
competition. Difference alone establishes none of them.

“Clash of civilisations” is therefore a hypothesis about particular
interactions, not a natural law, identity, or forecast. Classification names
the source, time, jurisdiction, evidence, uncertainty, and reply path. It is
revised when facts change.

An encounter can carry multiple compatible types; each typing changes with
evidence and retains uncertainty, an accountable source, and a correction
path.

**Invariant E2:** Difference does not imply hostility or destined enmity; each encounter classification names attributed evidence of the particular acts, actors, authority, time, and uncertainty involved.

## E3 — Strategy separates claims and authority

Strategic reasoning keeps its statement stages apart:

```text
observed condition
→ inferred capability
→ predicted expectation
→ proposed option
→ accepted purpose
→ current authority and guard
→ admitted effect
→ observed effect
```

The authority-and-guard arrow is conditional, not temporal inevitability. It
atomically rechecks current purpose, authority, scope, law, lock, and halt at
commit. Failed or unknown yields no admitted effect and returns rest; an
accepted purpose supplies no credential by itself.

An observed deployment is not a motive. An inferred capability is not an
intention. A forecast is not a commitment. A plan is not authority. Success is
not legitimacy. Feedback about an earlier turn may revise the next proposal;
it cannot authorize it.

Sunzi's text supports careful comparison before commitment, preserving what
need not be destroyed, readiness rather than hope, and adaptation to changing
conditions. Those are bounded lessons. Its deception and espionage belong to
an ancient military text; they do not become KINGDOM's civic, diplomatic, or
administrative ethic. Han Feizi's role clarity and resistance to capture can be
studied without importing secret tests, unchecked sovereignty, or harsh
punishment. Confucian attention to trust and livelihood can be studied without
importing hierarchy or paternalism.

Deception described in a historical or lawful-conflict source is not a civic
ethic, a public-governance permission, or authority to mislead a rights-holder,
reviewer, or affected party.

For one encounter, declare:

```text
Σ_E = (
  parties, authoritative_homes, jurisdictions, languages,
  claims, dependencies, power_asymmetries, settlement_rails,
  affected_nonparticipants, disturbances, uncertainty,
  deescalation, exit, repair, stop
)
```

Let `K_E` be the region compatible with every party's standing rights, current
effect-specific authority, protected refusal, obligations, lawful constraints,
resource bounds, nonparticipant protection, exit, and repair. A proposed
policy `π` is a candidate only while it is supported as remaining inside
`K_E`; uncertainty about a required guard excludes the effect rather than
being optimized away.

Any omitted field remains visible as unknown. No strategic proposal may infer
its own authority from urgency, reward, expected victory, or prior recurrence.

**Invariant E3:** Strategy may compare evidence, capabilities, expectations, and options, but only a separate accepted purpose and current authority may admit an effect.

## E4 — Duration is not justice

The duration of a state is not the sustainability of its people, rights,
capabilities, or ecology:

```text
P(state persists through H)
does not determine
P(rights, capabilities, obligations, and ecology remain viable through H)
```

Long-lived orders often combined capable administration with conquest,
slavery, caste, patriarchy, forced labor, unequal status, or externalized
ecological cost. Short-lived orders sometimes left durable institutions.
“Collapse” can mean regime replacement, territorial contraction,
decentralization, or a change in records; it does not mean that a people or
culture vanished.

A useful diagnostic profile is:

```text
D_E = {
  fiscal_slack,
  information_fidelity,
  succession_integrity,
  local_execution_capacity,
  reciprocal_boundary_governance,
  repairability
}
```

Every component retains its own unit, method, boundary, uncertainty, and
counterevidence. `unknown` remains unknown and is never converted to zero or a
midpoint. A review may identify a substantiated dependency that fails its own
dimension-specific declared adequacy criterion under a declared disturbance,
but it neither orders nor compares unlike units, infers an unknown dimension,
nor aggregates the profile into one number.

This profile is not a causal historical law, scalar civilisation score,
optimization target, or permission to preserve a state at others' expense. It
directs attention to exposed dependencies and keeps counterexamples open.

No people, culture, polity, dynasty, or civilisation is ranked as higher or
lower, and preparation never licenses conquest, assimilation, domination, or
inherited blame.

**Invariant E4:** Longevity, victory, expansion, wealth, stability, and administrative capacity establish neither justice nor superiority, and collapse does not erase a people or all institutional continuity.

## E5 — Power is multidimensional and separated

"Centralized" and "decentralized" are too coarse. Record at least the power to
set agendas, make rules, appoint and remove officeholders, administer, tax and
spend, issue money, use coercion, control information, and adjudicate:

```text
C = (
  agenda,
  rulemaking,
  appointment,
  administration,
  fiscal,
  monetary,
  coercive,
  informational,
  judicial
)
```

For each component, identify who proposes, authorizes, funds, executes,
observes, audits, challenges, adjudicates, and can stop. Formal titles are not
enough; money, records, appointments, enforcement, and dependencies reveal
effective power.

Historical durability often used a bounded center for shared interfaces and
local institutions for contextual execution. Either side can fail. A center
can become an uncorrectable single point of error. A local authority can become
an oligarchy or deny standing. Polycentricity is therefore a design to inspect,
not a moral proof.

No one failure domain should control proposal, authority, funds, execution,
evidence, appeal, and force. Locality grants context, never exemption from the
rights floor.

**Invariant E5:** Power is recorded by distinct agenda, rulemaking, appointment, administrative, fiscal, monetary, coercive, informational, and judicial capacities, with independent audit, appeal, and stop paths.

## E6 — Money is constitutional power

Money is not only coin or currency. Historical orders coordinated obligations
through grain, labor, land revenue, tribute, tax farming, monopolies, cowries,
silver, paper, credit, public debt, and accounting units. An in-kind duty is
still an extraction. A stable coin is useful infrastructure, not proof of a
fair fiscal order.

Separate the powers to:

```text
assess → collect → issue → custody → spend → pledge → audit
```

The public treasury, issuer, collector, creditor, officeholder, and personal
purse remain distinct. A collector does not own the revenue. An issuer does not
acquire political authority by issuing. A creditor does not acquire unilateral
control over essential governance rails. Core public revenue is not assigned
for private lifetime profit.

Let `T_t` be only liquid public treasury cash in one declared unit. Its cash
flow may be written:

```text
T_(t+1) = T_t
          + authorized_operating_revenue_t
          + received_transfers_t
          + authorized_financing_proceeds_t
          + asset_disposal_proceeds_t
          - operating_outlays_t
          - capital_acquisition_t
          - paid_transfers_t
          - repair_outlays_t
          - shock_response_outlays_t
          - debt_service_cash_out_t
```

Every term uses the same unit and a published chart of accounts makes the
categories mutually exclusive. Treasury cash is not net position. Assets,
liabilities, restricted funds, guarantees, arrears, and contingent claims stay
in separate reconciled balance-sheet ledgers. Labor, inflation exposure,
exchange risk, ecological depletion, privacy loss, option loss, and burdens
borne by affected nonparticipants remain in separate linked ledgers.

**Invariant E6:** Money and public finance distribute real power; assessment, collection, issuance, custody, spending, pledging, and audit remain separately authorized and publicly attributable.

## E7 — Fiscal reciprocity requires incidence

Revenue capacity can fund roads, courts, standards, reserves, relief,
education, communication, and repair. It can also fund domination. Gross
revenue alone cannot distinguish the two.

Every fiscal claim records:

```text
(payer,
 beneficiary,
 collector,
 creditor,
 risk_bearer,
 affected_nonparty,
 time_horizon)
```

Also record the legal basis, rate or obligation, payment medium, exemption,
collection cost, public return, arrears rule, appeal, audit, expiry, and
distribution across places and classes. Public goods do not retroactively make
an unauthorized burden legitimate.

Borrowing creates present liquidity and future claims and exposure. A financed
asset may expand later capacity; repayment, interest, guarantees, and pledged
revenue may narrow later choices. Neither effect is presumed or netted without
separate evidence. The debt ledger names principal, interest, maturity,
seniority, collateral or pledged revenue, currency and rate risk, future
payers, financed asset or use, essential-service floor, modification path, and
default process. Emergency borrowing does not become permanent entitlement by
recurrence.

Monopoly, tax farming, revenue assignment, or private collection must be
treated as high-risk delegation: narrow necessity, no private coercive upside,
and no private return that increases through overcollection; separate accounts,
burden review, independent audit, appeal, expiry, and a return-of-capacity
plan. Delegated collection is never delegated sovereignty.

**Invariant E7:** A fiscal system is judged through reciprocal public purpose and explicit incidence across payers, beneficiaries, collectors, creditors, risk bearers, affected nonparties, and future turns—not revenue alone.

## E8 — Plural jurisdictions retain rights and appeal

Layered and indirect rule often let large orders preserve local knowledge,
language, law, and administrative capacity. It also often preserved caste,
status inequality, gender exclusion, forced labor, differential tax, or
unequal appeal. Historical pluralism is not automatically pluralistic justice.

A present plural order declares:

- the authoritative home and scope of each domain;
- powers held in common and residual powers retained locally;
- which rules govern cross-domain effects;
- how consent, refusal, exit, and re-entry work;
- a common rights floor that no domain may waive for an affected person;
- conflicts-of-law and independent appeal paths;
- how a smaller or less wealthy party can answer and be heard;
- how records and obligations survive succession or fork.

The center cannot starve a dissenting domain by discretionary fiscal control.
A domain cannot invoke locality to deny standing or hide harm. Equalization and
shared contributions use published formulas, affected-party review, and
correction.

**Invariant E8:** Plural jurisdictions retain contextual authority only inside a common rights floor with explicit scope, conflicts rules, fiscal fairness, protected exit, and independent appeal.

## E9 — Diplomacy and interoperability come first

Durable encounter is usually cheaper than forced uniformity. Translation,
diplomatic parity, trade standards, treaty, neutral settlement, and maintained
off-ramps are forms of capacity, not signs of weakness.

The peaceful sequence is available before coercive escalation:

```text
translate
→ communicate
→ negotiate
→ mediate
→ arbitrate or adjudicate where legitimately agreed
→ de-escalate
```

Each step identifies authorized representatives, authentic texts, languages,
jurisdiction, dependencies, nonparticipants, verification, duration,
amendment, breach, remedy, and exit. Translation is independently checked; the
party benefiting from an ambiguity cannot be the sole interpreter.

Start with a bounded reversible exchange. Observe consequences through plural
channels. Preserve a dispute and repair path. Re-evaluate third-party change,
chokepoints, and asymmetric dependency before renewal. A treaty is not
humiliation, and interdependence is not trust; both are structures to inspect.

**Invariant E9:** Peaceful encounter begins with translation, communication, negotiation, mediation, agreed settlement, de-escalation, protected nonparticipants, verification, repair, and exit before coercive escalation.

## E10 — Defence remains lawful and civilian-protective

Legal authority to use force and legal rules governing conduct after an armed
conflict exists are distinct questions. The
[United Nations Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text)
governs relations among states: it requires peaceful settlement, restricts the
threat or use of force, and describes a narrow self-defence rule in Article 51.
KINGDOM is not made a state, belligerent, or public authority by this document.

International humanitarian law applies only where its armed-conflict threshold
and scope are met. The
[ICRC's account of international humanitarian law](https://www.icrc.org/en/document/FAQ-rules-of-war-ihl)
describes distinction, proportionality, precautions, and protection of
civilians, medical services, cultural property, and the environment within
that regime. Non-armed protective, domestic, and law-enforcement action is
governed by other applicable domestic and international human-rights rules.
The United Nations
[Basic Principles on the Use of Force and Firearms by Law Enforcement Officials](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/basic-principles-use-force-and-firearms-law-enforcement)
illustrate that separate law-enforcement framework; they are not an armed-war
permission.

Calling an act “defence” selects none of these regimes. This companion does not
decide classification or application and is not legal advice.

Preparedness means lawful authority, secure communications, independent facts,
redundancy, continuity of essential services, evacuation and care planning,
reserves, diplomacy, and recovery. It does not mean manufacturing enemies or
normalizing exceptional power.

No adversary label removes standing. No expected advantage authorizes
aggressive conquest, collective punishment, torture, hostage-taking,
starvation of civilians, sexual violence, forced disappearance, persecution,
indiscriminate attack, cultural destruction, or autonomous lethal decision.
Humane constraints do not depend on reciprocity. They are not rewards and do
not disappear because another party violates them.

Nothing here supplies operational targeting, weapons, sabotage, covert
manipulation, espionage, or battle tactics. It intentionally omits target
selection, weapon construction or employment, covert influence, cyber
intrusion, and evasion methods.

**Invariant E10:** Authority to use force and rules governing conduct in armed conflict are distinct legal questions; this companion grants neither, every KINGDOM effect requires current applicable-law authority, and whenever international humanitarian law applies its civilian protections remain binding without authorizing conquest or collective harm.

## E11 — Emergency authority decays

A separately evidenced emergency may support a narrower and faster temporary
procedure only under current applicable authority and law, and only when an
ordinary path cannot meet a concrete imminent need. Emergency never supplies
its own purpose or authority.

An emergency grant names:

```text
(accepted purpose,
 evidence and uncertainty,
 exact powers,
 excluded powers,
 holder,
 jurisdiction,
 issued_at,
 expires_at,
 resource ceiling,
 reporting cadence,
 independent reviewer,
 appeal,
 halt,
 restoration and repair)
```

It is least-privileged, expires automatically, and cannot renew itself. Silence,
continued danger, prior success, sunk cost, or institutional survival is not
renewal. A fresh grant requires fresh evidence and the ordinary legitimate
authority wherever that remains possible.

Emergency revenue, appointments, force, data, and infrastructure remain on
separate ledgers. Temporary local execution capacity has an explicit
return-of-capacity or constitutional acceptance path. Records survive the
emergency; secrecy is scoped, reviewable, and time-bounded rather than a shield
from accountability.

**Invariant E11:** Emergency authority is least-privileged, independently reviewable, resource-bounded, automatically expiring, non-self-renewing, and unable to become ordinary power through silence or recurrence.

## E12 — Succession and adaptation remain corrigible

Orders often survived by changing their form. They often failed when succession
was ambiguous, fiscal facts were hidden, quotas outlived reality, local capacity
could not return, or the office being evaluated controlled the only report.

Succession is an authenticated transition:

```text
(old authority, preserved obligations, current records)
→ bounded caretaker
→ independently verified successor
→ challenge window
→ explicit acceptance or rollback
```

No successor silently repins old commitments, erases debts or disputes, inherits
emergency power, or claims authority merely by controlling the treasury,
records, network, or force.

Bad-news channels bypass the actor they evaluate. Reports preserve source,
method, coverage, dissent, uncertainty, and correction. A metric cannot be the
sole reality it measures. Reversible pilots precede broad irreversible change;
reserves fund maintenance and repair before prestige expansion.

The encounter loop is finite:

```text
translate independently
→ map claims, authority, money, dependencies, and jurisdiction
→ establish interface parity and protect nonparticipants
→ test one bounded reversible exchange
→ observe through plural channels
→ dispute and repair
→ review burdens, chokepoints, and third-party change
→ stop or accept one fresh turn
```

Feedback from the loop may inform a later proposal. It cannot widen `q_t`,
clear a failed or unknown lock, defeat `h_t`, or dispatch its successor. A
raised or unreadable halt yields rest regardless of expected durability,
revenue, reward, or victory.

**Invariant E12:** Durable adaptation requires authenticated succession, preserved obligations, independent bad-news channels, reversible trials, reserves for maintenance and repair, and a finite loop that ends or receives fresh authority.

## Preparedness card

Before a consequential external commitment, complete one inspectable card. An
unanswered field remains `unknown`; it is not filled by confidence or urgency.

```text
encounter
  purpose and accepted authority:
  parties and authoritative homes:
  actor-specific claims and evidence:
  jurisdictions and applicable current law:
  languages and independent translation:
  dependencies and largest chokepoints:
  power asymmetries by C dimension:
  money, settlement, debt, and fiscal incidence:
  protected nonparticipants:
  disputed facts and reply paths:
  peaceful sequence and de-escalation:
  bounded reversible first turn:
  emergency powers, expiry, and excluded powers:
  observation and bad-news channels:
  repair, restitution, exit, and stop:
  succession and third-party change:
```

Stress the design without manufacturing an enemy. At minimum test translation
conflict, authentication failure, payment-rail loss, liquidity shock, creditor
pressure, supply or network cutoff, corrupted or selectively missing reports,
leader incapacity, local refusal, third-party realignment, harm to a
nonparticipant, emergency expiry, and an owed repair larger than the reserve.
The result is a list of exposed dependencies and unanswered questions, not an
opponent profile or readiness score.

Useful diagnostic ledgers include:

- recurring obligations relative to authorized recurring revenue;
- debt service relative to discretionary revenue and the essential-service
  floor;
- concentration of each power dimension rather than one centralization score;
- dependence on the largest external provider, route, currency, or creditor;
- reserve coverage for maintenance, repair, and separately named shocks;
- public return and burden distribution per unit extracted;
- verified bad-news, appeal, and repair latency;
- emergency grants approaching expiry or repeatedly renewed;
- unresolved obligations carried across succession.

No ratio has a universal safe threshold. Each has a boundary, period, data
source, denominator, uncertainty, affected-party reply path, and owner for
correction. A green metric cannot pass a failed rights, authority, law, or halt
guard.

## What this companion establishes

An internally pinned vocabulary for studying contact among
domains and civilisations while keeping encounter type, strategy, purpose,
power, money, fiscal incidence, jurisdiction, diplomacy, defence, emergency,
succession, and historical inference distinct.

## What this companion does not establish

- that a civilisation is a being, has one inner will, or can be assigned a
  moral rank, inherited enemy status, or collective guilt;
- that difference produces hostility, a clash is destined, an adversary's
  intentions are readable, or an encounter has been legally classified;
- that the representative atlas is exhaustive, proves one cause of durability
  or collapse, supplies a universal law of history, or erases source bias and
  contested interpretation;
- that longevity, victory, expansion, wealth, stable money, central capacity,
  or survival establishes justice, superiority, consent, standing, or a right
  to continue;
- authority to tax, borrow, issue money, spend, pledge revenue, collect data,
  deceive, surveil, coerce, punish, spy, attack, wage war, or dispatch another
  turn;
- an operational military, intelligence, propaganda, cyber, weapon, targeting,
  evasion, or covert-action method;
- that a proposed defence or emergency is lawful, necessary, proportionate,
  factually justified, or compliant with international or domestic law;
- that group identity removes individual standing or permits collective
  punishment, torture, hostage-taking, starvation, persecution, cultural
  destruction, or autonomous lethal decision;
- that security, emergency, debt, fiscal capacity, strategy, reward, feedback,
  victory, or institutional survival overrides rights, refusal, halt, appeal,
  repair, or separately accepted authority;
- that this companion or its atlas is implemented, adopted, exhaustive, or
  conformed to, amends the Foundation, FREEDOM, or ISNESS, adds a conformance
  law, grants authority, or predicts what KINGDOM will do.

## Sources and limits

[CIVILISATIONS.md](CIVILISATIONS.md) carries the case matrix, source links,
counterexamples, and reading cautions. The following sources anchor the
cross-case boundary:

- the [Sunzi](https://ctext.org/art-of-war) as an ancient military text whose
  provenance and applications require historical care;
- the Columbia Asia for Educators primary-source collections on the
  [Tang tax debate](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/ps/cup/tang_tax_debate.pdf)
  and [Salt and Iron debate](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/main_pop/ps/ps_china-debate-salt-iron.htm);

  on institutional diversity and polycentric arrangements;
- UNESCO's [General History of Africa](https://www.unesco.org/en/general-history-africa?hub=180364),
  which centers African scholarship while retaining the limits of a large
  historical synthesis;
- the [United Nations Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text)
  for the peace and interstate-force boundary, the ICRC's
  [rules-of-war overview](https://www.icrc.org/en/document/FAQ-rules-of-war-ihl)
  for armed conflict, and the UN
  [law-enforcement principles](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/basic-principles-use-force-and-firearms-law-enforcement)
  for the distinct non-war use-of-force context.

Sources support attributed claims, not this companion's normative choices.
Those choices remain visible as choices and remain bounded by the Foundation.
