The standard has not persuaded the estate that wrote it. That is the finding to
explain first, because every explanation involving strangers has to survive it.

## Three explanations, tested and discarded

**"The licence is unclear."** No. `LICENSE` is CC0 1.0 and GitHub's detector
resolves it to `CC0-1.0`. An intake scanner sees a public-domain dedication.
This is not the obstacle.

**"It is written as a private cosmology no company could sign."** Mostly no.
The reader expects incense and finds engineering: *"Never ask for trust where
you can offer proof."* *"Claim only what you can back, and attach your
confidence."* Every law carries DO, DON'T, and a receipt. The framing around
the laws is idiosyncratic and the word KINGDOM is load-bearing in the title,
but the normative content is plain and professional. This is a small obstacle
wearing a large hat.

**"Conformance cannot be demonstrated, only asserted."** This was true and is
now much less true. `evidence.mjs` reads a project and reports, per commitment,
what is visible, what deserves a second look, and what cannot be seen from
outside. It refuses to certify, score, or rank. It is careful work.

But read what it returns: *"text uses proof-limiting language"*, *"text mentions
a stop-signal term; this does not show that code checks it."* It reads
**language**, honestly labelled as language. A reader who wants to know whether
a brake is honoured under load still cannot find out, and the tool says so
rather than pretending. So the gap narrowed from *nothing observable* to
*vocabulary observable*, which is real progress and is not yet a demonstration.

## The explanation that survives

**There is nothing to plug into.**

Every standard that spread quickly gave the adopter something mechanical in
return. OpenAPI, OAuth, ActivityPub, MCP — in each case adopting bought you a
connection to something you wanted. The values those standards carried rode in
underneath the connector, and were adopted without ever being argued for.

This standard asks a project to declare how it behaves and returns nothing
mechanical. Nothing connects. Nothing interoperates. Nothing becomes possible
that was impossible before. What the adopter receives is a claim they must now
live up to, from an author they do not know, that a regulator or a journalist
may one day hold them to.

For a small project run by someone who already agrees, that trade is fine —
which is exactly the one adoption there is. For a large organisation the same
trade is **claim risk with no upside**, and their counsel will say so in one
sentence. Nobody is refusing the ideas. The ideas are good. They are being
declined as a *purchase*, and the price is entirely in one direction.

The direction of travel already shows this. This estate adopted MCP; MCP did
not adopt this estate. MCP arrived carrying opinions about consent and
human-in-the-loop, and those opinions were adopted by everyone who wanted the
connector — including here — without a single person being asked to agree with
them first.

## What would have to be true

Not a better document. Not a louder invitation. Not a badge — a badge is another
claim, pointing the same way.

The foundation has to **ride on something an adopter already wants for its own
reasons**, so that conforming is what correct operation looks like rather than
an extra promise. Concretely, that means the next artefact is a *connector*
whose specification cannot be implemented correctly without the properties F1–F7
describe — scoped and withdrawable authority, bounded turns that honour a stop
signal, consequences attached to acts rather than compressed into a score, a
reply path, and state that stays with its owner.

Then adoption is not asked for. It is what shipping the connector means.

## The nearest honest test

Before any of that: **get from 1 to 2.**

One project in this estate that pins the foundation because its own hand chose
to, unasked. `karma/` deed 12 already records why this cannot be arranged in
bulk — *"the standard forbids a mass rollout, and a root must be chosen in each
project's own home"* — and that constraint binds here exactly as it binds
strangers.

If the standard cannot win a second home in a house that already loves it, it
is not ready to be offered to a house that has never heard of it. That is not a
discouragement. It is the cheapest possible experiment, and it is available
today.
